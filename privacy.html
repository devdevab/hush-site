Privacy Policy

Hush collects no data. None.

Hush plays music files stored on your Mac. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information, usage data, analytics or crash reports, and it contains no advertising or tracking.

Your playlist, equalizer settings and the folders you choose are saved only on your Mac, in the app's own settings. They never leave your device.

Hush only goes online in two cases, and only when you ask:

  • Track Credits: the song's title, artist and ISRC code are sent to MusicBrainz, a free, open music database, to fetch who worked on it (MusicBrainz privacy policy).
  • Best Tracks: album and artist names are sent to Deezer's public catalogue to see which songs are most popular (Deezer privacy policy).

Nothing that identifies you is sent. How often you play or skip songs is counted only on your Mac, to help pick your best tracks, and never leaves it.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
