Privacy Policy

Hush collects no data. None.

Hush plays music files stored on your Mac. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information, usage data, analytics or crash reports, and it contains no advertising or tracking.

Your playlist, equalizer settings and the folders you choose are saved only on your Mac, in the app's own settings. They never leave your device.

Hush only goes online when you open Track Credits for a song. It then sends that song's title and artist to MusicBrainz, a free, open music database, to fetch the credits. Nothing that identifies you is sent. See the MusicBrainz privacy policy.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
